## What to learn

Build picture-book depth with static paper assets and coded camera motion.

**Try it yourself** · Editorial suggestions based on public source material, not a complete record of the creator’s process.

1. Approve an eight-scene script and prepare layered transparent assets.
2. Use Python for depth, parallax, blur and shadows; inspect stills for each scene.
3. Add narration, music and short sound-effect captions after camera motion.

## What to learn

Selecting tense events from real replays connects a trailer’s rhythm to gameplay.

**Try it yourself** · Editorial suggestions based on public source material, not a complete record of the creator’s process.

1. Choose real replays and mark near misses and gameplay-revealing moments.
2. Arrange shots and emphasis text on the beat without hiding key events.
3. Export Japanese and English copy, checking readability, timing and gameplay communication.

Tools mentioned in the source：Opus 5.5 · HyperFrames

## What to learn

Real screenshots and assets from a product site make a technical product film more concrete.

**Try it yourself** · Editorial suggestions based on public source material, not a complete record of the creator’s process.

1. Prepare your product URL and pick pages that show the core feature.
2. Animate site assets or screenshots in JavaScript, then record the result.
3. Check screenshot accuracy, music timing and the final video.

Tools mentioned in the source：JavaScript · Playwright · ffmpeg

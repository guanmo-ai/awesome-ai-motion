## What to learn

Problem, offer, process, proof and name can make a business legible in 30 seconds.

**Try it yourself** · Editorial suggestions based on public source material, not a complete record of the creator’s process.

1. Fill in the offer, audience and brand colors, and gather one real proof point.
2. Script five scenes with bold text and smooth transitions in one HTML page.
3. Play the 30-second cut; check reading time and the accuracy of the proof.

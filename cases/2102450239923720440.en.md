## What to learn

Joining the surface and underwater view in one interactive scene makes a miniature world worth exploring.

**Try it yourself** · Editorial suggestions based on public source material, not a complete record of the creator’s process.

1. Map the island, dinosaurs and underwater areas.
2. Build a single-page Three.js/WebGL scene and connect the above- and below-water views.
3. Exercise interactions in a desktop browser; check water gaps, occlusion and performance.

Tools mentioned in the source：Three.js · WebGL · HTML · Chrome

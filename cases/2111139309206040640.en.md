## What to learn

Give each new area one defining action and build fishing as separately playable systems.

**Try it yourself** · Editorial suggestions based on public source material, not a complete record of the creator’s process.

1. Build casting, the tension bar and catch cards as a separate loop.
2. Handle scenery, water and character rigs separately while reusing the farm HUD.
3. Define fish species, rarity and prices before playtesting and tuning.

Tools mentioned in the source：Opus 5.5 · Unity 6 · Blender · Tripo · ElevenLabs · Cinemachine

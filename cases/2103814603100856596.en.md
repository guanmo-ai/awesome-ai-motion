## What to learn

Use a product’s own sounds as rhythmic material and let the explanatory visuals follow.

**Try it yourself** · Editorial suggestions based on public source material, not a complete record of the creator’s process.

1. List the keyboard structures and actions to explain and choose switch recordings.
2. Arrange the real sounds into a rhythm, then plan 3D actions and cameras.
3. Check the relationship among each action, sound and label, then watch the full sequence.

Tools mentioned in the source：Opus 5.5 · Three.js

## What to learn

Choosing a lyric treatment before timing each line to the song makes text the star of a vertical MV.

**Try it yourself** · Editorial suggestions based on public source material, not a complete record of the creator’s process.

1. Prepare the song and checked lyrics.
2. Sketch vertical title cards first, then align each line to the vocal.
3. Watch and listen to the full video for timing, overlap and legibility.

## What to learn

Treat a building image as separate object and camera problems before planning its exploded animation.

**Try it yourself** · Editorial suggestions based on public source material, not a complete record of the creator’s process.

1. Match the camera and main building structure to the reference.
2. Separate cladding, glazing, frames and furniture into objects with procedural materials.
3. Plan exploded paths, check intersections and revise movement.

Tools mentioned in the source：Opus 5.5 · Blender · EEVEE

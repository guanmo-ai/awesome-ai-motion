## What to learn

Explicit morphing and rendering constraints give scene changes a consistent rhythm and continuity.

**Try it yourself** · Editorial suggestions based on public source material, not a complete record of the creator’s process.

1. Map the beats and specify how one shape introduces the next scene.
2. Compute frames from time and implement morphs and glass effects in passes.
3. Inspect beat frames and single-frame changes around transitions, then watch the full film.

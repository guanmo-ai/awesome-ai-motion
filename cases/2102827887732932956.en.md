The original voice, captions and duration are retained while the main footage becomes animation.

## What to learn

Keeping the speaker's audio while replacing the main view with topic-matched line art makes an explainer more visual.

**Try it yourself** · Editorial suggestions based on public source material, not a complete record of the creator’s process.

1. Prepare your own talking-head clip and its captions.
2. Put the speaker in a lower-right inset and draw the discussed concepts in the main view.
3. Check concept timing, caption clearance and unchanged audio/duration.

**Creator’s public prompt** · [Source](https://x.com/AxtonLiu/status/2102827887732932956) · [Plain text](../prompts/2102827887732932956.txt)

```text
把 short-1.mp4 做成一条新的竖屏短片 short-1-v5.mp4：

1. 我的人像缩小成右下角的圆形画中画，能看清我在讲话，不遮字幕。
2. 主画面换成一段动画 B-roll，跟着我讲的内容走：我说到哪个概念，画面就画哪个概念。风格轻松有趣，线稿动画就可以，不要写实。
3. 原声、原字幕、时长都不变。
```
